## Usage

Dividers are used to visually separate content, adding clarity and structure to a layout. They are often used to distinguish between sections of text, groups of components, or different functional areas on a page. They help guide the user's eye, reinforce hierarchy, and improve readability by breaking content into smaller segments.

## Variants & Modifiers

### Orientation

The divider is only available in horizontal orientation. We currently don't offer vertical dividers or dividers with different widths or styles in Gemini.
