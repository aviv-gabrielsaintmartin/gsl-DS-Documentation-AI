## Usage

Cards are used to group related content and actions into a visually distinct, cohesive container. They help structure information and provide an organized layout.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/31c903cc435aed09c0276f?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T130901Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=a708df0af94337a32fa6d16ddef8e03251c27a853db817645fa5ab36456a8d34)

Do

Use cards to encapsulate related content or actions that belong together. You can add text, actions, icons, illustrations and images within cards. Place elements in a way that creates a clear hierarchy and is easy to scan.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/51d5299c88c8b000e83f3f?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T130901Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=e333c548d120c5932fdf94e734ec1911ad74e42c86bdeba216ff63e227f8bf28)

Do

Separate larger cards with a divider.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/0cde26c1018209be5a6da0?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T130901Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=f1b388a8774cbe0ec0716d8c2284c6a28d052da78b9270ddc017fe51b801f9a2)

Do

Create clickable cards by placing the cell content component inside the card.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/61b6a75b40707a9a8f65b2?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T130901Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=9348046192dd5656ea2c0e60276fb265f7d236fb3c2eb32884ac3dfe907ae27d)

Don't use cards for selection. Use select cards instead.

## Behaviors

### Interaction

Cards themselves are not clickable. If you want to create clickable cards, place a [cell content](https://zeroheight.com/626199550/p/27116a-cell-content) inside the card.

  

![Card with padding](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/4f47fb33f283ec615ac4cd?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T073752Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=3fc94e16d633ae399c04ee6502c779755532622afeeb8a65b01e28fab2b05479)

Card with padding

Add notes

Only the cell content itself is clickable and has interactive states. If the card has padding, that part is not clickable.

![Card without padding](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/10bcadae13998ec99a8cc6?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T073752Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=2b455458911f5dbcd002354e72e8a76040fda88df96875fbbd49a3348e7f6b4b)

Card without padding

Add notes

If you want the whole card to be clickable, use it without padding. The overlapping parts of the cell content will be cropped by the card.

### Width and height

The width and height of the card are determined by its contents, with the card adapting to the elements inside.
