### Best practices

  

**Limit the number of lines to 4 or 5 to maintain clarity**

Exceeding this limit may result in a confusing "spaghetti chart" with excessive line overlaps.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/f9108e125c6b333b74c94e?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T132249Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=51aae984b84e2081e1de963b783d2e99ae32a377100906c20b5127877d41d976)

**Use a baseline different than 0 accordingly to your data**

It is not required for a line chart to use a 0 baseline.The primary purpose of a line chart is to highlight value changes, not the actual values.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/e13345a24dc2f7166feb52?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T132249Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=e7bf1606ac2f0df38cc449f9edc4371ace79170bc60795ec7b6227b98e425fd1)

**Be careful about the width/height ratio as it can affect how trends are perceived in line charts.**

A broader chart can create the impression of smoother trends, while a more compact chart emphasizes data fluctuations. It's important to consider how design choices impact the interpretation of the chart.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/a7b450b8545273bfb819b8?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T132249Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=4ced46d4bd33c384eea1e9d872fdd5aabb251bbd2f41aac2518ba463b9c836c0)
