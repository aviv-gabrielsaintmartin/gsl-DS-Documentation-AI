## Usage

Energy tags serve as a visual indicator to quickly communicate the energy efficiency of a property. Each tag consists of a letter and a color code, providing a standardized, at-a-glance indicator of a building's energy performance.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/ce59970630d0d71fe178ce?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T131811Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=c2efbb43a8e8a97ef091a57abdc6efacc057468927609f5ee3e7e5f701e3a52e)

Do

Use energy tags in property-related interfaces to allow potential buyers or renters to consider energy performance in their decision making.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/b1ba138b531dc540f4b14f?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T131811Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=d07ee0399cb3a135fd49d0efc7138abcee23f9bc1513edb578fbf045269719f8)

Don’t

Don't use energy tags to represent non-energy ratings, such as reviews, quality scores or customer satisfaction levels.
