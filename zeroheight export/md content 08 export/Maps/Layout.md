# Layout

Map experiences at AVIV vary across products and domains, but by providing a common layout, we establish a consistent pattern, helping users become familiar with finding different elements on the map.

![](/uploads/W1qO5fIyIe3C6HV1R8e3HA.png)

## Usage

Creating a cohesive and intuitive layout for a map experience involves following a set of design rules to ensure that users can easily interact with the map and access necessary controls across products and domains. Here's a guide to layout rules for your map experience.

![Layout for main interactions](/uploads/B7_l4piE0UO7g04WGZWFsg.png)

Layout for main interactions

-   **Primary and secondary actions:** The primary actions are positioned at the bottom center of the map. These actions slide downward when the user interacts with other elements on the map, such as the contextual information card. They slide back up once the card is dismissed.
    
-   **Map controls**: The controls for navigating and operating the map are positioned in the bottom right corner. For desktop devices, the zoom control is mandatory. Additional controls depend on the specific experience and are stacked vertically above the zoom control.
    
-   **Map layers control**: Controls for managing map layers are positioned in the top right corner. The button can either be a standalone icon button or a labeled button that opens a panel, allowing users to select different layers of map information.
    
      
    

![The bottom center can hold up to two actions](/uploads/7yhxVtotvh2jfNlsCOXTBQ.png)

-   **Additional elements:** Additional elements, such as a legend or extra filters, can be positioned in the top left corner of the map. However, be mindful of the space these elements occupy to ensure users have adequate room to interact with the map effectively.
