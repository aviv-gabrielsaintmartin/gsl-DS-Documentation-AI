## Usage

Tags are non-interactive labels used to display information or status that cannot be edited or changed by the user. They are typically used to provide context, categorize or highlight important attributes of an item.

![](https://zeroheight-uploads.s3-accelerate.amazonaws.com/e2d2ec26806cad8562a56c?X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=AKIA3AVNYHQKWXNYIVGF%2F20260901%2Feu-west-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260901T133208Z&X-Amz-Expires=86400&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Signature=5e01fa009abd274c12e8b59e92c0d7928bedd18fe9deb436c67effb403c8a097)

Do

Use tags to display static, non-interactive labels to categorize and highlight items.

### Related components

**Component**

**Usage**

Tag

Tags are non-interactive components used to display fixed information such as labels, categories, or statuses. They are purely informational and do not allow user interaction.

[Chip](https://zeroheight.com/626199550/p/32f686-chip)

Chips are dynamic, clickable components that allows user interaction, such as selecting, filtering, or removing items.

Score tag

Score tags are non-interactive components used for the seller lead scoring.

## Content

Tag labels should be clear, concise, and specific to effectively convey their purpose.

Use sentence case without punctuation.

Try to keep it to 2-3 words and about 20-30 characters in English.

For more information on content guidelines, please refer to the [UX Writing principles](https://zeroheight.com/626199550/p/324518-intro).
